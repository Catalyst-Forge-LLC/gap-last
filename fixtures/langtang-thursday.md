## New trace

Clearer satellite pass. North-face rock avalanche on Langtang Lirung,
ice included, then a long runout. Source:
https://www.onmanorama.com/news/world/2026/08/28/satellite-images-reveal-massive-bedrock-collaps-nepal-floods.amp.html

Shugar: a big bedrock failure that took part of the glacier with it.
Cook: the rock collapsed; the glacier was cargo. Bedrock under the
glacier tongue failed on the north face (~5,200 m). Mass dropped into
Lendi/Lhende Khola; debris flood; river went green to brown (sediment
load). Villages and border works buried downstream.

Onmanorama and the Cook/Shugar interviews do not mention construction
at the scar. Climate enters as possible preconditioning (weakening
permafrost "glue"), not as the precise trigger of this face.
