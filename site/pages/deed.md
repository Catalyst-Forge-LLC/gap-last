Visible without an account. This is the human form. The skill and the CLI keep the same order.

## Four-line deed

For a person with no tool running:

1. The what, as tightly as you can say it. Mark it.
2. One mechanism the geometry, timing, or record already kills.
3. The leftover gap, named as a question.
4. Whether "how" or "who caused it?" is even the right question yet.

## One-screen

When you have one breath, or when `/full` prefixes the artifact:

1. The what (bound), marked settled / provisional / first-noun risk.
2. Stories already killed.
3. Open gaps, as questions.
4. If the bound moved: old leftover versus new leftover.

A one-screen summary must not smuggle a hypothesis that is missing from the allowed-hypothesis list.

## The reconstruction

The file is the format. Nine sections, that order only. A hypothesis that does not point at a named residual gap is illegal. [Template](https://github.com/Catalyst-Forge-LLC/gap-last/blob/master/docs/reconstruction-template.md). [Spec](/spec).
